# About Pedro Campos

Pedro Henrique Campos Moreira is a Machine Learning Engineer with more than 3
years of experience in AI, focused on Conversational and Generative AI. He has
developed and shipped more than 14 chatbots and virtual assistants powered by
RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) and LLMs to automate customer service for
clients across many sectors. He also has 5 award-winning research papers.

He is based in Belo Horizonte, MG, Brazil. His toolkit includes the Claude API,
OpenAI API, LangChain, Dify, Botpress and Dialogflow, along with prompt
engineering, knowledge-base curation, and analysis of real user interactions to
drive improvements.

Pedro also has experience in customer service (Itaú) and in communicating AI to
non-technical audiences through articles, a mini-course, and teaching. He is in
the final semester of his Information Systems degree at UFV, expected to
graduate in December 2026.
