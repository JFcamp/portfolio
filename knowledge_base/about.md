# About Pedro Campos

Pedro Henrique Campos Moreira is a Machine Learning Engineer with more than 3
years of experience in AI. He works across the full spectrum of machine
learning: computer vision, classical ML, generative AI, MLOps, and conversational
AI, taking systems from prototype to production. He also has 5 award-winning
research papers in deep learning.

His experience spans several areas: computer vision (weed-segmentation with
YOLO, on-device/edge inference), classical machine learning (explainable credit
scoring with calibration and fairness), MLOps (model registry, monitoring,
CI/CD, drift-triggered retraining), generative AI and RAG, and applied AI for the
financial market. In conversational AI he has shipped more than 14 chatbots and
virtual assistants to production. His toolkit includes PyTorch, TensorFlow,
Scikit-learn, YOLO, ONNX, MLflow, Docker, FastAPI, LangChain, and the OpenAI and
Claude APIs.

He is based in Belo Horizonte, MG, Brazil. Pedro also has experience in
communicating AI to non-technical audiences through articles, a mini-course, and
teaching, plus early experience in customer service (Itaú). He is in the final
semester of his Information Systems degree at UFV, expected to graduate in
December 2026.
